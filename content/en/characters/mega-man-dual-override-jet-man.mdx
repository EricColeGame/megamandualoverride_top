## Boss Fight Prep: Beating Jet Man

Bosses that fly are rarely about damage output. They're about arena control — forcing you into a corner and then filling that corner with something. The Mega Man Dual Override Jet Man fight will almost certainly reward players who move deliberately and punish players who panic-jump.

| Phase | Likely tell | Recommended response |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Boss hovers at mid-screen, testing your position | Stay grounded, keep distance, learn the dash timing |
| Aggressive | A wind-up before a horizontal charge | Slide under or jump the charge, never retreat into a wall |
| Aerial | Boss rises to the top of the arena | Track it with a vertical-capable weapon; stay mobile |
| Desperation | Faster, less telegraphed repeats of earlier moves | Stop attacking, focus entirely on dodging until the pattern resets |

Actionable preparation for the Mega Man Dual Override Jet Man boss fight:

- **Learn the dash first.** Aerial bosses almost always have one signature movement move. Everything else is a variation on it.
- **Find the weakness before you grind.** The classic weakness chain means one weapon usually cuts this fight in half. Test the roster you already have before brute-forcing it.
- **Practice the slide.** Low-profile movement beats jumping against horizontal charges in nearly every game in the series.
- **Watch the arena, not the boss.** Flight patterns take the boss off-screen or into corners. The room tells you where the next attack is coming from.
- **Reset instead of tilting.** If you've lost more than half your health before the second phase, restart. A clean run teaches you more than a desperate one.
